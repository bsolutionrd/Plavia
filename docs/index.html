Features

Search, browse and play everything on YouTube Music — wrapped in a fast, glassy interface with serious audio underneath.

Search, explore & play

Search, browse and play anything available on YouTube Music, with a local music library alongside it.

Hi-Res lossless audio

Lossless Hi-Res playback, with codec, bit depth and sample rate stats right on the now-playing screen.

Gapless with crossfade

Gapless playback with true crossfade, adjustable from 0 to 12 seconds, plus skip silence and speed control.

Automix

DJ-style transitions with beat-matching and tempo-stretching, so the music never really stops.

Offline downloads

Save tracks with embedded metadata and keep listening with no connection at all.

Word-by-word lyrics

Synced lyrics with word and syllable-level highlighting, and an Apple-like lyrics animation.

Sign in with Google

Sign in with your Google account for personalized content, your library and your recommendations.

Spotify

Connect your Spotify account to play your playlists and Liked Songs inside Plavia.

Discord Rich Presence

Show the live track, artist, album and progress on your Discord profile.

Scrobbling

Scrobble everything you play to Last.fm and ListenBrainz.

Only in Plavia

The things you won’t find in the original.

Play without VPN

Keep listening even with a VPN active: playback binds to the direct network (Wi-Fi or mobile data) instead of the VPN, so the stream is not refused.

Settings → Playback → Play without VPN

Local premieres first

Home opens with premieres from your country, then worldwide premieres — new music from home, first.

Liquid Glass throughout

Frosted-glass bars, mini player, cards and sheets across the whole app, with the audio output selector right in the player.

Wear OS companion

The watch app shows what’s playing with play, pause, next and volume — and installs straight from the phone app.

Settings → Plavia for Wear OS

Download

Plavia is distributed as a signed APK through GitHub Releases — no app store, no waiting for reviews.

1

Get the APK

Grab the latest signed APK from the Releases page.

2

Allow the install

Sideloading requires enabling Install unknown apps for whichever app you download it with.

3

Install & play

Open the downloaded file, install it, and press play.
